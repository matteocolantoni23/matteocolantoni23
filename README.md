# Matteo Colantoni

I study how statistical models can make reliable decisions with limited computation. My research interests span discrete generative models, sequential decision making, optimization, and the gap between an ideal algorithm and one that must estimate its own inputs.

I completed a BSc in Mathematical and Computing Sciences for Artificial Intelligence at Bocconi University and am studying Financial Mathematics at LSE. In my bachelor's thesis with Giacomo Zanella, I studied data-driven schedules for masked diffusion models: estimating an information profile, bounding the cost of using that estimate, and proving that the exact finite-step schedule can be computed in $O(KN)$ using Monge structure.

### Selected work

- **[Data-driven schedules for masked diffusion](https://github.com/matteocolantoni23/masked-diffusion-schedules)** — thesis companion code for Gaussian information profiles, exact schedule optimization, and online scheduling experiments.
- **[Hotel recommendation systems](https://github.com/matteocolantoni23/hotel-recommendation-systems)** — collaborative and content-based recommendation, ranking evaluation, and context-aware hybrids on hotel reviews.
- **[Hopfield network simulation](https://github.com/katiarusso/stochastic-processes-simulation)** — collaborative course project on retrieval behavior in a stochastic associative memory.
- **[Single-cell hypoxia classification](https://github.com/katiarusso/breast-cancer-ml-classification)** — collaborative ML project on hypoxic versus normoxic breast cancer cells.

I am interested in research at the intersection of statistics, optimization, and machine learning, particularly when computational choices must be made using incomplete or noisy evidence.
